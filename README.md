## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we collect and process:

- **Account and identity data:** your email address, Firebase user ID, and authentication information. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google supplies the information needed to authenticate your account, such as your email address and Google account identifier.
- **User-created learning content:** course titles, topic titles, descriptions, lesson content, generated lesson files, and other material you create or upload through the App.
- **Learning and access data:** lessons you open, library-access records, quiz scores, and related progress or activity needed to provide learning features.
- **AI feature inputs:** questions submitted through in-page AI help and Studio instructions, including any lesson context sent with those requests. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information in AI prompts unless it is necessary for the feature.
- **Technical and security data:** Firebase authentication tokens, Firebase App Check attestation tokens, and technical information processed by our service providers to secure the App, prevent abuse, and operate the service. This may include IP address and device/network information processed by those providers.

The App does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or advertising ID.

## 3. Why we use data

We use the data above to:

- create and secure accounts and provide sign-in;
- store, synchronize, display, and manage courses, lessons, and learning activity;
- provide library access, interactive lessons, quiz scoring, and AI-assisted learning or authoring features;
- protect the App from fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
- troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the App; and
- comply with applicable law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.
