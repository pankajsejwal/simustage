# Privacy Policy for Simustage

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

## 1. Who we are

Simustage (the “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is operated by **Pankaj Sejwal**, in **India**. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data when you use the Simustage Android app and related services.

For privacy questions, contact **support@simustage.com**.

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we collect and process:

* **Account and identity data:** your email address, Firebase user ID, and authentication information. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google supplies the information needed to authenticate your account, such as your email address and Google account identifier.
* **User-created learning content:** course titles, topic titles, descriptions, lesson content, generated lesson files, and other material you create or upload through the App.
* **Learning and access data:** lessons you open, library-access records, quiz scores, and related progress or activity needed to provide learning features.
* **AI feature inputs:** questions submitted through in-page AI help and Studio instructions, including any lesson context sent with those requests. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information in AI prompts unless it is necessary for the feature.
* **Technical and security data:** Firebase authentication tokens, Firebase App Check attestation tokens, and technical information processed by our service providers to secure the App, prevent abuse, and operate the service. This may include IP address and device/network information processed by those providers.

The App does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or advertising ID.

## 3. Why we use data

We use the data above to:

* create and secure accounts and provide sign-in;
* store, synchronize, display, and manage courses, lessons, and learning activity;
* provide library access, interactive lessons, quiz scoring, and AI-assisted learning or authoring features;
* protect the App from fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
* troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the App; and
* comply with applicable law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. How data is shared

We share data only as needed to operate the App:

* **Google Firebase / Google Cloud:** Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, and Firebase App Check provide account authentication, data storage, server-side features, and security protections. These services process relevant account, content, access, and technical data on our behalf.
* **Google Sign-In / Credential Manager:** if you select Google Sign-In, Google processes authentication information needed to sign you in.
* **AI processing:** Studio and in-page AI requests are sent to our Firebase Cloud Functions service. To generate responses and assist with content creation, these functions securely transmit your specific questions, prompts, and relevant lesson context to major third-party AI service providers, which may include **OpenAI, Google Cloud (Gemini), and Anthropic**. These providers process your requests on their servers to return the AI-generated output. Under our API agreements with these providers, your data is processed solely to provide the requested AI feature; it is not used to train their foundational AI models. These providers retain API data only temporarily (typically up to 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring before securely deleting it.
* **Legal and safety recipients:** we may disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or the public.

We require service providers to process data only for the purposes described here and under appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

## 5. Storage, security, and international transfers

We use HTTPS for data in transit and access controls provided by Firebase and Google Cloud. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our service providers may process data in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 6. Retention

We keep account data, learning content, and activity data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the App. When you confirm an account-deletion request, we permanently delete the associated account data and content immediately. We do not retain account data, learning content, access records, quiz scores, or AI inputs after deletion, except where retention is strictly required for a lawful purpose such as security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## 7. Your choices and account deletion

You can update content in the App and sign out at any time.

To request deletion of your account in the App, open the three-dot menu and choose **Delete account**, then confirm the request. This is intended to delete your Simustage account and associated data, including authored courses and library-access records.

You can also request account deletion outside the App at simustage.com or by contacting **support@simustage.com**.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your data, or to request a copy of it. Contact us at **support@simustage.com** to make a request.

## 8. Children

Simustage is an educational service intended for people of all ages, including children. We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide accounts, learning features, and security as described in this Policy. We do not sell personal data or use children’s personal data for targeted advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal data without the required permission, contact us at **support@simustage.com**. We will review the request and, where appropriate, delete the child’s account and associated data.

Children should use the App with parent or guardian involvement where required by applicable law.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version and update the “Last updated” date accordingly.
